# Hi, I'm Juan Ignacio Diaz Schneider 👋

**Experimental Physicist & Materials Researcher | PhD Candidate at the Balseiro Institute**[cite: 1]

I specialize in the device physics and material science of memristive systems applied to neuromorphic computing[cite: 1]. My doctoral work focuses on the physical modeling, synthesis, and advanced characterization of transition metal oxides (TiO2) and silver nanowire networks[cite: 1]. I combine nanoscale experimental research with the development of custom instrumentation and software for testbed automation[cite: 1].

### 🔬 Research Interests
* Implementation of neuromorphic computing tasks in physical learning systems[cite: 1].
* Transport phenomena and control of resistive switching mechanisms in silver nanowire networks[cite: 1].
* Statistical evaluation of non-volatile memory reliability (cycle-to-cycle variability, endurance, and state retention)[cite: 1].

### 🛠️ Technologies & Development
* **Hardware Development:** Design of custom multi-terminal acquisition systems (PAN Board) utilizing common ESP32 microcontrollers and PCB design[cite: 1].
* **Software & Simulations:** Implementation of physical transport models and testbed automation using advanced Python (NumPy, SciPy)[cite: 1].
* **Advanced Characterization:** Impedance spectroscopy, Conductive AFM (C-AFM), and pulsed I-V sweeps under strictly controlled environments[cite: 1].
