# Hi, I'm Juan Ignacio Diaz Schneider 👋

**Experimental Physicist & Materials Researcher | PhD Candidate at the Balseiro Institute**

I specialize in the device physics and material science of memristive systems applied to neuromorphic computing. My doctoral work focuses on the physical modeling, synthesis, and advanced characterization of transition metal oxides (TiO2) and silver nanowire networks. I combine nanoscale experimental research with the development of custom instrumentation and software for testbed automation.

### 🔬 Research Interests
* Implementation of neuromorphic computing tasks in physical learning systems.
* Transport phenomena and control of resistive switching mechanisms in silver nanowire networks.
* Statistical evaluation of non-volatile memory reliability (cycle-to-cycle variability, endurance, and state retention).

### 🛠️ Technologies & Development
* **Hardware Development:** Design of custom multi-terminal acquisition systems (PAN Board) utilizing common ESP32 microcontrollers and PCB design.
* **Software & Simulations:** Implementation of physical transport models and testbed automation using advanced Python (NumPy, SciPy).
* **Advanced Characterization:** Impedance spectroscopy, Conductive AFM (C-AFM), and pulsed I-V sweeps under strictly controlled environments.
